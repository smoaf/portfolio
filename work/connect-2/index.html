CONNECT 2

My role at LEWITT

As Product Manager I was responsible for CONNECT 2, from definition through development to the market launch in May 2024. Across other projects, among them headphones, microphones, audio interfaces and VST plug-ins, I worked in audio engineering, testing and UX design. I researched product categories, what users need in them, and how to innovate and find new solutions.

The product

CONNECT 2 is a USB-C audio interface for musicians, content creators and anyone with a dedicated setup for gaming or meetings. Inputs for microphone and instrument and outputs for speakers and headphones, in both jack sizes, sit neatly on the back for a clean setup. Its form factor and touch controls rethink how an audio interface is used, and every function is designed to be easy to understand for anyone. The controls give clear visual feedback, and together with the CONTROL CENTER software they make good sound possible in a few clicks.

What it does

CONNECT 2 records one microphone and one instrument over USB-C. A ten-second soundcheck is enough for Autogain to set the input level, and Autosetup configures the remaining processing, so a recording sounds clean without any knowledge of gain staging.

  1. Finds suitable settings by itself, using the integrated Clipguard, Denoiser, Compressor and the three preamp sounds.

  2. Keeps the signal safe from clipping with one click, even when things get loud.

  3. Sets the input level after a ten-second soundcheck.

  4. Reduces background noise and hum with one click.

  5. A polished, even sound with one click.

  6. Clean for clarity, Warm for a vintage vibe and a smoother high end, Vivid for more articulation.

  7. Mute every channel on the device or in the software, or hold MUTE to silence everything at once.

  8. Assign a workflow shortcut, such as switching from recording to monitoring or opening CONTROL CENTER.

  9. Colour and brightness of all LEDs, set in CONTROL CENTER.

  10. Mix the input with the computer playback at any ratio. Effects run on the device's DSP, so monitoring has no latency.

  11. Captures the computer's playback audio, for samples, calls, streams or playing along.
