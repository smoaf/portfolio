## 12 Oct 2026: Workshop stations, phase W4 (server rack: Mind reader), branch `feat/workshop`
- `workshop/rack.js`: the existing bank of three racks on the left of the workshop (behind the glass partition) becomes the server rack. Middle rack: the main screen (MIND READER, round counter, both moves as pictures, the result, the score you / draws / rack, the "how well I know you" meter with a mark at chance, the rack's guess for the round just played as three confidence bars, its remark or the final verdict), below it a panel with three big keys ROCK / PAPER / SCISSORS and FORGET ME, and rows of LEDs that show the little network's hidden units. Side racks: two fans each at the top, a small screen (left "What I watch": every habit's hit rate, the one it trusts highlighted; right "Your last moves" with win/loss marks and the network's hidden units as dots), and rows of LEDs between the grille bars. Screens are drawn on canvases and lit from inside.
- The rack reacts: LEDs blink slowly at rest and flicker for a moment after each round while it "thinks" (trains), fans spin up as it gets confident, the screens repaint after each round.
- `workshop/rack-mind.js` (plain logic, no three.js): rock, paper, scissors over a match of 20 rounds. Before each round the rack asks six small predictors for a guess (favourite move; what follows the last move; what follows the last two; stay / step up / step down after a win, a loss or a draw; the answer to the rack's last move; a tiny network, 21 inputs → 10 tanh → 3, trained online after each round) and trusts the one with the best recent hit rate (faded record, about the last 7 rounds). It plays at random for the first 5 rounds and whenever no predictor beats chance, and stays a little random even when confident. Its move is fixed before the visitor picks; its guess is shown only after the round. Remarks are true facts about the visitor's play, picked by interest and not repeated back to back ("You've played rock twice after a loss.", "After a win you tend to stay with the same move.", "I guessed rock, so I played paper. I watched what you play after each move."); if it can't get ahead after 12 rounds it says "You're unpredictable, respect." After 20 rounds a verdict ("I win 11–5 … Your tell: …"); the next move starts a new match with the habits kept. Simulated over 200 matches each: a random player stays at chance (33/33/34); a player with fixed habits (always mostly rock, cycling, win-stay/lose-shift, beating the rack's last move) loses 73–97 % of rounds 11–20; a noisy "human-like" bot about 25/56/19.
- Controls: the 3D keys (click or tap), the bar along the bottom (Rock 1, Paper 2, Scissors 3, Forget me; the remark repeated there in readable size), keys 1/2/3 or R/P/S, F forgets. Nothing is stored or sent; it is all forgotten when the visitor leaves the workshop.
